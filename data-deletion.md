# Data Deletion Instructions for AI Content Agent

**Last Updated: September 27, 2026**

AI Content Agent provides users with a way to request deletion of information associated with their use of the service.

## How to Request Data Deletion

**qweasdzxc432188@proton.me**

Use the subject:

**AI Content Agent Data Deletion Request**

Please include sufficient information to identify the relevant account or data associated with your request.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, App Secrets, or other authentication credentials.

## Verification

For security and privacy purposes, we may request reasonable information to verify that the person making the request is authorized to request deletion of the relevant data.

## What We Will Do

After a valid deletion request is verified, we will identify information associated with the request and delete or anonymize information that is no longer required for operation of the service.

Certain limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for:

- security and fraud prevention;
- audit and system integrity;
- duplicate-publication prevention;
- compliance with applicable legal obligations;
- establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

Any retained information will remain subject to appropriate security controls.

## Facebook and Meta Data

Where the request concerns information obtained through Meta APIs, AI Content Agent will process the request in accordance with applicable Meta Platform Terms and policies.

Deleting information stored by AI Content Agent does not automatically delete information independently stored by Facebook or Meta. Users may need to use Facebook or Meta's own account and content controls for data maintained directly by Meta.
