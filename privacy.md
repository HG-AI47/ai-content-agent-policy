# Privacy Policy for AI Content Agent

**Last Updated: September 27, 2026**

AI Content Agent ("we", "our", or "the Service") is a content management and publishing tool used to assist with managing content for Facebook Pages that the authorized operator has permission to manage.

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Content Agent accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when interacting with Meta and Facebook services.

## 1. Information We Access

AI Content Agent may access information made available through the Meta APIs according to permissions granted by an authorized Facebook user.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include:

- Facebook Pages that the authorized user manages;
- Page identifiers and Page names;
- Page access permissions and assigned tasks;
- Page posts, videos, Reels, and related publishing information;
- Engagement information necessary to verify or manage Page content;
- Publishing and processing status returned by Meta APIs.

AI Content Agent does not request access to Facebook passwords.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Meta APIs is used only to provide content management and publishing functions for authorized Facebook Pages.

This may include:

- identifying an authorized Facebook Page;
- preparing content for publication;
- publishing posts, videos, or Reels;
- checking publishing and processing status;
- verifying that content was published to the intended Page;
- preventing accidental duplicate publishing;
- maintaining operational and audit records necessary for reliable publishing.

We do not sell Facebook user data or Page data.

We do not use information obtained through Meta APIs to create advertising profiles or to sell personal information to third parties.

## 3. Authorization

AI Content Agent only performs Facebook Page operations using permissions granted by an authorized Facebook account with the appropriate Page access.

Access is limited to the permissions approved through Meta's authorization process.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens used by AI Content Agent are stored using security controls designed to prevent unauthorized access.

Operational records may be retained when necessary for security, troubleshooting, duplicate-prevention, auditing, and reliable publishing.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent information obtained through Meta APIs.

Information may be processed by service providers only when necessary to operate the Service, maintain infrastructure, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of the Service.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including operational, security, audit, and legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.
